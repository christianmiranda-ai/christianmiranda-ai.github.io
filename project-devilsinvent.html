Devils Invent 2025 Competition: 1st Place Finish


Summary

  • ADAPT leverages additive manufacturing for modular, efficient UAV engines.
  • Optimized with nTop for thermal management, noise reduction, and performance.
  • Reduces costs, lead times, and complexity while improving efficiency and durability.
  • Positions for growth in the expanding UAV market.

Project Overview and Design Principles

The goal was to design a lightweight, modular, and rapidly producible small propulsion engine (characterized as less than 1000 lb-f of thrust) to reduce logistical burdens and enable field reparability. The engine was constrained to a 400mm x 400mm x 375mm print volume (typical for an EOS M400-sized LPBF printer) while balancing performance, material constraints, and cost-effectiveness for the war fighter.

AM-optimized design consolidates static components for streamlined performance, using industry-standard Nickel-based alloys (e.g., IN718, IN625) for maximum strength. Engineered with quality inspection workflows (e.g., 3D scanning, CMM) in mind, it's fully compatible with LPBF technology like EOS M400s. From over 150 parts to only 15, components are sized for print efficiency, re-engineered to be self-supporting, with wall thickness redistributed to minimize warping and eliminate powder entrapment.

Topology optimization with field-driven software like nTop reshapes parts for maximum efficiency. Complex geometries are enabled by advanced 3D printing. nTop creates lighter, stronger designs tailored to real-world conditions.

Metal Additive Manufacturing takes advantage of various cutting-edge design principles: Integrated Thermal Management, Acoustic Attenuation, Topology Optimization. With nTop, we designed innovations like thermal management, sound reduction, and topology optimization.

Using nTop, we created an integrated cooling channel system within the casing, routing cold air from the diffuser and aspirated inlet guidance vanes for transpiration cooling of the stator vane and turbine.

Additionally, the lattice structure is optimized for 3D folded core acoustic lining to capture and attenuate noise, reducing the sound profile.

Lastly, nTop optimizes the combustion process and inlet guidance vane geometry to reduce pressure drop at the inlet and improve the flow path. This engine has the potential to exceed performance expectations.

Radiative Air Cooling and Design Features

Air entering the compressor at 400°C is cooled via radiative panels with niobium, yttrium, and copper.

Design emphasizes: Lightweight Design, No Circulating Fluids or Pumps, Maximized Heat Transfer with nTop, Higher Combustion Chamber Temperature, Simplicity & Robustness.

Simplicity: Reduce complexity, minimize failure points, simplify manufacturing and repair. Replacement parts can be easily printed and replaced in the field.

Reduced Lead Time: Rapid prototyping and direct production shorten development cycles.

More Efficient Design: Freedom of design enables lighter, optimized geometries with improved performance (e.g., cooling, weight reduction, vibration damping).

Unlocking Capital: Significant cost savings and new revenue streams from adopting additive manufacturing at scale.

Performance Improvements

  • High/Low Cycle Fatigue: As-printed Inconel alloys comparable to cast methods in durability.

  • Fuel Efficiency: Optimized inlet, outlet, and flow design improves efficiency by 15%.

  • Cooling Improvements: Better thermal management prolongs engine life and efficiency.

  • Noise Reduction: Acoustic attenuating geometry reduces noise by at least 5%.

Economic and Market Advantages

Low upfront capital—Honeywell already pays for the tools! Saves over $800k from tooling for casting, complex sheet metal forming. Less man hours per engine for fabrication and assembly. Massive waste reduction: Metal powder is reusable, Honeywell receives discounts for recycling metal scrap, less material than subtractive manufacturing.

Unmanned Aircraft expected to grow by 50% - 90% in the next 5 years. Current casting processes have lead times over 52 weeks. Additive manufacturing delivers lighter, cooler, more efficient engines in weeks instead of months.

Long processes relying on repeated testing, verification, and certification. Complex assemblies mean long and expensive maintenance times. Making changes is slow and difficult.

With nTop, topology optimization reshapes parts for efficiency, generating organic shapes only possible with 3D printing. Creates parts perfectly adapted to their environment, lighter and more durable. Complex internal channels for transpiration cooling, sound attenuation, and higher load capacity.

Iteration moves at the speed of thought with additive tools. GOM Inspect: Geometric Deviation Analysis. Simufact Additive: Thermomechanical Warpage Simulator.
