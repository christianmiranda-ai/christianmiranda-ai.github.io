Habitation and Advanced Deployment for Exploration of Subterranean Systems (HADES) Mission


This project was hosted through the NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy (MCA) Program. In it, 15 of us worked over the course of months to develop a three-rover system capable of exploring lunar lava pits. In doing so, we learned all about how NASA approaches developing systems for its missions. This included, but was not limited to learning mechanical design principles to risk analysis to triple constraint navigation (programmatics) and more. When it was all said and done, our team was amongst the highest performing in the entire program!

Vehicle Design Summary:

  • Anubis Rover (Carrier): Designed (by a fellow teammate), this is a 40kg, 2m x 1.25m x 1.25m titanium chassis to house smaller rovers, with six-wheel rocker-bogie suspension for 30° slopes and magnetometer mounts for stable data collection.

  • Prometheus Rover (Scout): Designed by myself, this is a 25kg, 0.45m x 0.58m x 0.43m agile rover with dust-protected enclosures for RADOM dosimeter and six-wheel mobility for pit scouting.

  • Tsukuyomi Rover (Climber): Designed by myself, this is a 15kg, 0.25m x 0.38m x 0.19m design with LEMUR-inspired grippers for vertical climbing, housing LOLA LiDAR in a carbon-titanium frame.


Mechanical Subsystem Design

I developed integrated chassis, suspension, and mobility systems using titanium alloys for durability and regolith resistance. Key features include rocker-bogie suspensions for terrain navigation and adaptive grippers for climbing. I ensured compliance with requirements like 4.1kN load-bearing and thermal protection via FEA and material optimization.

Recovery and Redundancy

I incorporated redundancies (e.g., duplicated fasteners, sensor networks) and recovery plans, using piezoelectric gauges for real-time fault detection to ensure mission reliability in lunar vacuum.

Manufacturing and Procurement

I selected suppliers (Alcoa for alloys, Maxon Motors for actuators) and planned a 14-25 week timeline for procurement, fabrication, and QA, aligning with NASA standards.

Verification

I validated designs through CMM inspections, FEA for structural loads, and thermal cycling tests, achieving TRL 7 for mechanical subsystems.

Summary

  • Delivered mission-ready designs for a 2030 lunar launch under $300M budget.
  • Achieved TRL 7, enabling lunar pit exploration.
  • Mastered CAD, FEA, and systems integration.
  • Led a top-performing team, enhancing my aerospace expertise.
